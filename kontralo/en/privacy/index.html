Privacy policy for Kontralo

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Controller

Khaled Hashimi (Ruhelo), An der Lingenmühle, 41061 Mönchengladbach, Germany
Email: ruhelo.apps@gmail.com

2. In short

  • Kontralo stores your contracts, documents and settings encrypted on your device.
  • There is no user account and no server run by us. We do not receive your data and cannot see it.
  • Kontralo contains no ads and no analytics or tracking services.
  • Installation, updates and purchases are handled by Google Play (see section 5).

3. Data in the app

Everything you enter or scan in Kontralo (e.g. contracts, providers, amounts, deadlines, notes, scanned letters, photos and PDF files) is stored only on your device – in an encrypted database and an encrypted file store. The keys are kept in your device's protected key store (Android Keystore).

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Kontralo. The letter scanner works entirely on your device; photos are not transmitted. Deadline reminders are calculated and shown on your device.

If you uninstall Kontralo, this data is deleted from your device. Without a backup of your own it cannot be restored afterwards – not even by us.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: only for scanning letters when you open the scanner. The images stay on your device.
  • Notifications: for reminders about notice periods and backups.
  • Fingerprint/face (biometrics): only if you turn on the app lock. Android performs the check; Kontralo receives no biometric data.
  • Internet and network state: needed to buy Kontralo Pro through Google Play (Google Play Billing). Kontralo itself does not transmit the content of your contracts or documents.

5. Google Play

Kontralo is distributed through Google Play. For downloads, updates and purchases (Kontralo Pro, redeeming codes), Google processes data under its own responsibility, e.g. your Google account and payment information. We do not receive payment data from Google, only whether a purchase exists.

The purchase library required by Google (Google Play Billing) may send technical diagnostic data about the purchase process to Google. Google also provides us with aggregated, anonymous statistics about crashes and app performance if you have agreed to share usage and diagnostics data in your Android settings. We cannot identify individuals from these statistics.

More information: Google Privacy Policy.

6. Backup, export and sharing

Only you start a backup, an export or sharing of documents, and you choose the storage location or target app (e.g. Downloads, Google Drive, email). Backups are encrypted with your password; we do not know this password. The export (table and documents) is not encrypted – the app tells you before you start it. Data you pass on to other apps or services is subject to their privacy policies.

7. Contact by email

If you write to us by email, we process your email address and the content of your message only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete the message once it has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

8. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs in order to deliver and protect the site. The website uses no cookies, no analytics and does not load fonts or content from third parties. Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. As we do not receive any data from the app, this usually only concerns emails you send us. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.

10. Changes

If Kontralo changes (e.g. through new features such as synchronisation with your own cloud), we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
