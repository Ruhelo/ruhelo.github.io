Help us test Kontralo.

An app for contracts and notice periods – free, no ads, your data stays on your phone. Before it goes to the Play Store, we’re testing it together.

How to join – in two steps

You need an Android phone and the Google account you use for the Play Store.

1. Join the tester group

Open the Google group and tap “Join group”. The group only tells Google who may test – you won’t get any advertising through it.

Open group

2. Become a tester

Then open the test link and tap “Become a tester”. After that, install Kontralo from the Play Store as usual.

Open test link

Please keep Kontralo installed for at least 14 days – the test has to run that long before Google releases the app for everyone. Thank you!

Your thank-you

As a tester you’ll get Kontralo Pro as a gift later. As soon as Pro is available, we’ll send you a code by email.

Feedback

What do you like, what doesn’t work? Write to ruhelo.apps@gmail.com or use “Send feedback to the developer” in the Play Store.

Privacy

This page sets no cookies and doesn’t count visitors. Kontralo itself has no ads and no tracking either – more in the privacy policy for Kontralo.
